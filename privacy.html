Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Budget Buddy
The short version: Budget Buddy stores your financial data securely using Firebase (Google). We never sell your data, never share it with advertisers, and you can delete your account at any time. Read on for the full details.
1

Information We Collect

When you use Budget Buddy, we collect only what is necessary to make the app work for you:

  • Your name and email address (used for account creation and login)
  • Financial data you enter — salary, expenses, savings targets, and payment reminders
  • Notification preferences you configure
  • Custom categories you create for expense tracking

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any data beyond what you explicitly enter.

2

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and sync your expense tracking and budgeting features across devices
  • To send you payment reminders and notifications (only if you enable them)
  • To restore your data if you reinstall or change devices
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs — we never use your financial data for this
3

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase — a cloud platform used by millions of apps worldwide. Firebase encrypts data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.

Access to your data is protected by Firebase Authentication. Only you, with your credentials, can access your account. Budget Buddy staff cannot read your financial entries.

4

Third-Party Services

Budget Buddy uses the following Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication — secure login and account management
  • Cloud Firestore — storing your financial data
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — sending push notifications

These services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not use any advertising or analytics SDKs.

5

Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • You may request a copy of your data at any time
  • You may request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • You may opt out of notifications at any time via Settings

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

6

Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you via the app before those changes take effect. Continued use of Budget Buddy after that date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions about your privacy?

We're happy to help. Reach out to the Budget Buddy team.
