Features

Everything you need to stay on top of your money — nothing you don't.

Built for real people tracking real budgets. No spreadsheets, no complexity.

📊
Smart Dashboard
See your remaining budget, monthly spend, top spending category, and upcoming payments the moment you log in. Everything at a glance.
💸
Expense Tracking
Log expenses by category with notes. Edit or delete anytime. Add custom categories for anything that doesn't fit the defaults.
🕐
Expense History
Filter all past expenses by category or month. Full monthly summaries show budget used, remaining balance, top category, and total savings.
🔔
Payment Reminders
Track SIPs, subscriptions, and bills. Push notifications alert you before they're due. Monthly payments auto-roll to the next cycle.
🎯
Savings Planner
Set a goal, enter your salary and savings amount — Budget Buddy tells you exactly how many months it takes and shows a full timeline milestone by milestone.
➕
Extra Funds
Got pocket money, a freelance payment, or a friend paid you back? Log extra income separately so your budget stays accurate without touching your salary.
🔄
Monthly Carry Forward
Any unspent balance at month end automatically carries forward to the next month. Your leftover money never disappears.
🌍
Multi-Currency Support
Track finances in INR, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, MYR, LKR and more. Switch currency anytime from settings — every number across the app updates instantly.
🏷️
Custom Categories
Add your own spending categories beyond the defaults. Rename or remove them anytime. Past expenses stay untouched when a category is removed.
